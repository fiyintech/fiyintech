### 🎓 Sign Out — Digital Graduation Platform

A platform inspired by a real problem surrounding Nigerian secondary-school graduation sign-out activities.

Traditional "sign-out" activities allow graduating students to have friends and classmates sign their shirts with messages and well wishes. After incidents surrounding student misconduct caused some schools to reconsider the activity, I explored how the same social experience could be recreated digitally.

Sign Out allows graduating students to have a personalized digital board where friends and family can leave signatures and messages, including people who cannot physically attend.

**Built with:**

* Web technologies
* Frontend development
* Database systems
* Product design

**Status:** Completed

**Repository:** Deployment Paused

### 🌐 ORA — Social Platform for Nigerians

A social platform concept designed around Nigerian culture while maintaining a modern, professional digital experience.

ORA experiments with culturally familiar concepts such as:

* **Hoods** instead of traditional group chats
* **Gbedu** as a dedicated music and entertainment space
* **Aura Points** as a reputation and engagement system
* **Steeze Level** as a gamified social ranking

The project is an exploration of how software can be designed around the culture and communication patterns of its intended community rather than simply copying existing platforms.

**Status:** In active development

## 🧠 How I Learn

I believe the fastest way for me to understand something is to build with it.

When something breaks, I don't like stopping at the error message. I want to understand **why** it broke.

One of the challenges I encountered while developing and deploying applications was dealing with projects that built successfully but failed once deployed. Understanding environment variables, backend configuration, hosting environments, and the separation between development and production taught me that software engineering is about much more than writing code that compiles.

Sometimes a problem takes hours.

Sometimes it takes days.

I keep working until I understand it.
